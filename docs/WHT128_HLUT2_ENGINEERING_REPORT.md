### 1.2 Exact Failure Modes of Stock 2-Bit Formats & Mathematical Formulation (`WHT128-HLUT2`, Logical-First Permutation, & `778`-Expert `RAREBAL-LDLQ`)

| Candidate 2-Bit Format | Effective `bpw` | Exact Failure Mode Across `Blackhole P150A` (Tensix SRAM) & `MI50` (`gfx906`) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Stateful Bitshift Trellis ([`QTIP`](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.11235v1) / [`EXL3`](https://github.com/turboderp-org/exllamav3))** | `2.00 – 2.05` | Sequential LCG / bitshift state per weight creates loop-carried dependencies across `32`-wide [`Tensix SFPU`](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-isa-documentation/blob/main/BlackholeA0/TensixTile/TensixCoprocessor/VectorUnit.md) lanes, stalling `16`-subtile L1 circular-buffer unpacking by `> 6×`. |
| **Vector Lattice Codebooks ([`QuIP#` `E8P`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04396) / `IQ2_XXS`)** | `2.06 – 2.13` | Requires random `256`-entry `8D` codebook gathers per byte. Tensix `SFPLOAD` addresses tile rows rather than arbitrary per-lane scatter/gather indices, while `gfx906` `64`-lane wavefront gathers suffer `8–16`-way LDS bank conflicts. |
| **Unrotated Hardware `Bfp2_b` (`16`-datum shared exp)** | `2.50` | **(1) Numerical collapse:** Unrotated channel outliers collapse `SwiGLU` cosine to `0.729`. **(2) Blackhole LLK bugs:** Native `Bfp2_b` paths hit TT-Metal issues ([`#58712`](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/issues/58712), [`#56682`](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/issues/56682), [`#57683`](https://github.com/tenstorrent/tt-metal/issues/57683)). **(3) Capacity overflow:** Exceeds `160 GiB` DRAM by `24.6 GiB`. |
| **`WHT128-HLUT2` (Ours, [`PolarQuant` Lloyd-Max](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29078v1))** | **`2.125`** | **Zero LUT gathers, zero sequential state, zero `Bfp2_b` bugs:** Orthonormal `WHT128` Gaussianizes `128`-wide blocks and symmetric 2-bit centroids collapse into a **branchless 1-bit affine FMA** on both Tensix `SFPU` ($\to$ L1 `Bfp8_b` $\to$ `FP32` `Dst`) and `gfx906` (`v_dot2_f32_f16`). |

For each `128`-element block $b$ with deterministic Rademacher sign vector $s_b \in \{\pm 1\}^{128}$, orthonormal Walsh-Hadamard rotation $Q_b = \frac{1}{\sqrt{128}} H_{128} \operatorname{diag}(s_b)$ Gaussianizes weights to $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{r,g}^2 I_{128})$ (`0.125 bpw` scale), decoded via symmetric [`PolarQuant`](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29078v1) centroids (`c0 = 0.452780`, `c1 = 1.510417`) in the spirit of [`Thinking Machines Lab`'s Randomized Hadamard Transform](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/end-to-end-4-bit-training/) and [`QuIP#` `LDLQ`](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04396v1):

$$\hat{w}(s, m) = (-1)^s \cdot \sigma_{r,g} \cdot \bigl(c_0 + m \cdot (c_1 - c_0)\bigr), \qquad H_{\text{bal}} = \frac{H_{\text{common}} + \lambda H_{\text{rare}}}{M_{\text{cal}}} + 0.015\,\bar{d}\,I_K, \qquad \lambda = \frac{\operatorname{tr}(H_{\text{common}})}{\operatorname{tr}(H_{\text{rare}})}$$

In Wave 8.4:

* **Logical-First Bias Invariance (`cos = 1.000000000`):** `k_moe_gate` evaluates all `384` router logits, `ffn.gate.bias` / `ffn.gate.bias_vl`, and top-`6` weights strictly in **logical expert space `0..383`** before translating winning IDs through `uint16_t d_expert_perm[40 * 384]` (`30 KiB`) into physical `TP3` (`[0..215]`, `272 B/tile`) or `TP2` (`[216..383]`, `4,352 B` superblocks) slots (`445` swapped pairs, `565` base repacked experts, `max_diff = 2.980e-08` across 40 layers).
* **`778`-Expert `3× MI50` `FP64` `TARQ RAREBAL-LDLQ` (`0` Dual Receipt Violations):** Over a `646K`-token multi-domain + long-horizon corpus (`M_cal >= 10,240`, `M_eval = 512`), [`TARQ` `RAREBAL`](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27808) calibrates **`668` hot backbone experts** (including `100%` of `18/18` per-layer `P150A` L1 experts) plus **`110` `DSpark` `mtp.0..2` drafter experts** (`2.97×` Wave 8.3's `37`), requiring every emitted expert to beat `H = I` simultaneously on held-out SwiGLU cosine and rare-token MSE (`dual_receipt_violations = 0`; mean cosine `0.88527 → 0.95523`, **`+4.57 dB`** mean SNR overall, **`+5.45 dB`** mean SNR on `mtp.0..2`, `-63.0%` rare-token MSE).
