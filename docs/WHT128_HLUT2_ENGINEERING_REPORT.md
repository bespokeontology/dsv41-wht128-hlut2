### 1.3 Mathematical Formulation & Track 8C `561`-Expert `3× MI50` `FP64` `RAREBAL-LDLQ` Scale-Up

For each `128`-element block $b$ with deterministic Rademacher sign vector $s_b \in \{\pm 1\}^{128}$, orthonormal Walsh-Hadamard rotation $Q_b = \frac{1}{\sqrt{128}} H_{128} \operatorname{diag}(s_b)$ ([`QuaRot`](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/quarot) / [`SpinQuant`](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16406v4)) preserves inner products $\langle w_r, x \rangle = \langle \tilde{w}_r, \tilde{x} \rangle$ while Gaussianizing weights to $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_{r,g}^2 I_{128})$ (`BF16`/`FP16` scale $\sigma_{r,g} = 0.125\text{ bpw}$). Symmetric 2-bit centroids $\{-c_1, -c_0, +c_0, +c_1\}$ (`c0 = 0.452780`, `c1 = 1.510417`) decode via an exact 1-bit affine FMA:

$$\hat{w}(s, m) = (-1)^s \cdot \sigma_{r,g} \cdot \bigl(c_0 + m \cdot (c_1 - c_0)\bigr), \qquad H_{\text{bal}} = \frac{H_{\text{common}} + \lambda H_{\text{rare}}}{M_{\text{cal}}} + 0.015\,\bar{d}\,I_K, \qquad \lambda = \frac{\operatorname{tr}(H_{\text{common}})}{\operatorname{tr}(H_{\text{rare}})}$$

Note the structural convergence with [`Thinking Machines Lab`'s `End-to-End 4-Bit Training`](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/end-to-end-4-bit-training/), which applies a block-wise Randomized Hadamard Transform (`RHT`) inside its `w2` (`down_proj`) epilogue to suppress outlier spikes before low-bit MoE combine compression (`8.25 bpw` `S2F6`). `WHT128-HLUT2` applies deterministic `WHT128` across `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, and `down_proj` at `2.125 bpw`, backed by Causal Reverse-Cholesky [`QuIP#` `LDLQ`](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04396v1), post-quantized `Z_cal` Hessians on `down_proj`, and [`TARQ` `RAREBAL`](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27808) ([EMNLP 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.664/)) rare-token trace equalization.
